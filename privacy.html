GYMPLIFIED

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Gymplified is a fitness tracking service for recording workouts, nutrition, hydration, goals and progress. This policy explains what information the current Gymplified website and app experience handles and how it is used.

Launch note: This is the initial privacy-policy draft for the current product. Before public App Store or Google Play release, the operator's legal name, business address, contact email, final retention periods, and any future SDKs or integrations must be reviewed and completed.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, username, display name, account identifiers and email-verification status.
  • Profile information: optional profile picture, friend connections or requests, and your height, bodyweight and age if you choose to enter them.
  • Fitness information you enter: exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, workout sessions, personal records, goals, food entries, nutrition totals and hydration logs.
  • Purchase information: if you buy Gymplified Deluxe, whether your account owns Deluxe, the Stripe payment and checkout reference numbers, the amount and currency paid, and the dates of the purchase and of any refund or chargeback. Gymplified never receives or stores your card or bank details.
  • Support information: support-ticket subjects, categories and messages you submit, plus replies from Gymplified support.
  • App usage analytics: events such as page views, app opens and feature usage. The current first-party analytics records an account identifier when signed in, a session identifier and a limited feature/event label.
  • Technical information: information required by the hosting, authentication, database and storage services to deliver the app, protect accounts and diagnose failures.

2. Where information is stored

Some app state is stored in the browser or installed app on your device. If you sign in, Gymplified can sync your app progress to its Supabase-hosted database so it is available across devices. Account profiles and optional profile pictures are stored using Supabase services. The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. The app also loads its Supabase client library and fonts from external providers.

3. Why information is used

  • To provide sign-in, account recovery, email verification and cross-device syncing.
  • To calculate and display your workouts, nutrition, goals, records, streaks and progress reports.
  • To support friend connections and user-requested profile features.
  • To sell Gymplified Deluxe, unlock it on your account, restore it on request and remove it after a full refund or chargeback.
  • To answer support requests, investigate bugs and improve reliability and usability.
  • To understand which app areas are being used and improve the product.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Sharing and service providers

Gymplified does not sell personal information. Information is processed by service providers needed to operate the product, including GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for authentication, database, storage and related backend services. Those providers process information under their own service terms and privacy documentation. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect users and the service.

  • Stripe (Deluxe purchases): when you choose to buy Deluxe, Gymplified sends Stripe your account identifier and email address to open the checkout page. Deluxe is sold through Link, a Stripe service, which is the merchant of record. Stripe collects your payment details, billing country and any other details it needs at checkout, and uses them to take the payment, calculate tax, prevent fraud, provide purchase support and meet its legal obligations. Stripe handles this information as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy. Stripe tells Gymplified when a payment succeeds, is refunded or is disputed, so that Deluxe can be unlocked or removed.
  • Open Food Facts (barcode scanner): when you scan a food barcode, the camera image is processed on your device and only the barcode number is sent from your browser to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) to look up nutrition values. No account details or camera images are sent, but like any web request it reveals your IP address and basic browser information to Open Food Facts, which handles it under its own privacy policy. You can add foods by hand instead of scanning.
  • Your friends: if you accept a friend connection, that friend can see the information your privacy settings allow, such as totals, level, badges and recent personal records.

5. Your choices and controls

  • You can export your Gymplified progress using the backup and CSV export controls in Settings.
  • You can change or remove optional profile information in the app.
  • You can contact support through Settings → Help & Support.
  • You can permanently delete your account from Settings. The deletion flow removes the authentication account and associated account records, including the profile picture stored in the Gymplified avatars bucket. Local-only data on a device may need to be cleared separately.
  • You can use the app without enabling optional device notification permissions; notifications are not required for core tracking.

6. Retention

Account and synced fitness data are retained while the account remains active, unless a shorter period is required. When you request account deletion, the account and associated records are deleted through the account-deletion process. Your Deluxe purchase record in Gymplified is deleted together with the account. Stripe keeps its own records of the payment for as long as it is legally required to, for example for tax and accounting. Some limited records may remain temporarily in encrypted backups, security logs or provider systems according to their retention cycles and legal obligations. Data stored only on your device remains under your device/browser controls until you clear it.

7. Security

Gymplified uses Supabase authentication, email verification, access controls and database row-level security for supported account data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and use a unique password.

8. Children and age requirements

Gymplified is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children where parental consent is required by applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided information without required consent, contact the operator so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, restrict, object to or request deletion of your personal information, and to complain to your local data-protection authority. You can use the in-app controls or contact the operator to make a request.

10. International processing

Our hosting and backend providers may process information in countries outside your own. Before store launch, the operator should confirm the providers, processing locations and applicable transfer safeguards for the production configuration.

11. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Gymplified adds features or changes providers. The current version and update date will be published on this page. For privacy questions, use the in-app Help & Support feature. The operator's formal legal identity and contact email must be added here before public store release.
