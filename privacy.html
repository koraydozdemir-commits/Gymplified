GYMPLIFIED

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Gymplified is a fitness tracking service for recording workouts, nutrition, hydration, goals and progress. This policy explains what information the current Gymplified website and app experience handles and how it is used.

Who is responsible: Gymplified, operated from Ireland under the trading name Gymplified, is the controller of the personal information described here. Contact: gymplifiedsupport@gmail.com.

1. Information we handle

  • Account information: email address, username, display name, account identifiers and email-verification status.
  • Profile information: optional profile picture, friend connections or requests, and your height, bodyweight, body measurements, age and supplement checklist if you choose to enter them. Body information is optional, and some of it may be treated as health-related data, so we only handle it because you choose to enter it (see section 4).
  • Fitness information you enter: exercises, sets, repetitions, weights, workout sessions, personal records, goals, food entries, nutrition totals and hydration logs.
  • Purchase information: if you buy Gymplified Deluxe, whether your account owns Deluxe, the Stripe payment and checkout reference numbers, the amount and currency paid, and the dates of the purchase and of any refund or chargeback. Gymplified never receives or stores your card or bank details.
  • Support information: support-ticket subjects, categories and messages you submit, plus replies from Gymplified support, and any emails you send to our support address.
  • Optional anonymous analytics: only if you choose “Allow anonymous analytics”. Each event records an event name, a random per-session identifier and a broad feature label such as “workouts”. Your account ID is not sent with these events, so they are not linked to you. Analytics are off until you allow them and you can switch them off at any time in Settings → Privacy.
  • Technical information: information required by the hosting, authentication, database and storage services to deliver the app, protect accounts and diagnose failures, such as IP address and browser details in server logs.

Gymplified does not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage and a service-worker cache only to run the app, keep you signed in, save your settings and let the app work offline.

2. Where information is stored

Some app state is stored in the browser or installed app on your device. If you sign in, Gymplified syncs your app progress to its Supabase-hosted database so it is available across devices. The Gymplified database and file storage are hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Account profiles and optional profile pictures are stored using Supabase services. The website itself is served through GitHub Pages. The app’s Supabase client library and fonts are served from Gymplified’s own site, not from third-party content networks.

3. Why information is used

  • To provide sign-in, account recovery, email verification and cross-device syncing.
  • To calculate and display your workouts, nutrition, goals, records, streaks and progress reports.
  • To support friend connections, friend challenges and user-requested profile features.
  • To sell Gymplified Deluxe, unlock it on your account, restore it on request and remove it after a full refund or chargeback.
  • To answer support requests, investigate bugs and improve reliability and usability.
  • If you allow it, to understand which app areas are being used and improve the product.
  • To protect the service, prevent abuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Gymplified does not make decisions about you by solely automated means that have legal or similarly significant effects, and does not sell your information or use it for advertising or profiling.

4. Legal bases (GDPR)

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on the following bases:

  • Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)): creating and running your account, syncing your data, and delivering and restoring a Deluxe purchase.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)): optional anonymous analytics, and optional body information you choose to enter (bodyweight, measurements, age and supplements). Where any of this counts as health-related data, we rely on your explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a), given by choosing to enter it. You can withdraw consent at any time by turning analytics off, removing the entries in the app, or deleting your account. Withdrawing does not affect processing carried out before you withdrew.
  • Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)): keeping the service secure, preventing abuse and fraud, answering support requests and fixing bugs. You can object to this at any time (see section 9).
  • Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)): responding to lawful requests and keeping the records the law requires.

5. Sharing and service providers

Gymplified does not sell personal information. Information is processed by service providers needed to operate the product, including GitHub Pages for website hosting and Supabase for authentication, database, storage and related backend services. Those providers act as our processors under their own service terms and data-processing documentation. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to protect users and the service.

  • Stripe (Deluxe purchases): when you choose to buy Deluxe, Gymplified sends Stripe your account identifier and email address to open the checkout page. Deluxe is sold through Link, a Stripe service, which is the merchant of record. Stripe collects your payment details, billing country and any other details it needs at checkout, and uses them to take the payment, calculate tax, prevent fraud, provide purchase support and meet its legal obligations. Stripe handles this information as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, available at stripe.com/privacy. Stripe tells Gymplified when a payment succeeds, is refunded or is disputed, so that Deluxe can be unlocked or removed.
  • Open Food Facts (barcode scanner): when you scan a food barcode, the camera image is processed on your device and only the barcode number is sent from your browser to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org) to look up nutrition values. No account details or camera images are sent, but like any web request it reveals your IP address and basic browser information to Open Food Facts, which handles it under its own privacy policy. You can add foods by hand instead of scanning.
  • Your friends: if you accept a friend connection, that friend can see the information your privacy settings allow, such as totals, level, badges and recent personal records. If you own Gymplified Deluxe, friends also see a Deluxe (or Founding member) badge next to your name unless you turn it off in Deluxe → Style.
  • Friend challenges: if you join a challenge, the other members who are your friends see your workout count or training volume for the challenge dates, as your privacy settings allow. Members you are not friends with only see that one more person is taking part, never your name or numbers. Leaving or declining a challenge removes you from it.

6. Deleting your account (right to erasure)

You have the right to have your personal information erased, and you do not need to contact us to use it. Sign in, open Settings → Account → Your data rights, choose Delete my account permanently, and type DELETE to confirm. Erasure takes effect immediately and cannot be undone. We recommend downloading a backup first (Settings → Data).

What is erased straight away:

  • your sign-in account, email address, username and profile;
  • your synced progress, workouts, nutrition, hydration, goals, body information and settings;
  • your friend connections and requests, and the reactions you gave or received;
  • your support tickets and messages;
  • your Deluxe record held by Gymplified;
  • your profile picture, and any analytics events linked to your account;
  • the Gymplified data held in the browser or app you deleted from. Copies on other devices you used can be removed by clearing that site’s data.

What is not erased by deleting your account, and why:

  • Stripe’s payment records. Stripe is an independent controller and keeps its records for as long as tax and accounting law requires. Contact Stripe or Link support for requests about those records.
  • Backups and logs. Copies may remain in the encrypted backups and security logs of our hosting providers until they expire on the providers’ normal cycles. They are not used to bring a deleted account back, and they are not used for any other purpose.
  • Anonymous analytics you allowed, which contain no account identifier and so cannot be linked back to you.
  • Emails. If you wrote to our support email address, that correspondence sits in an email mailbox, not in the app. Ask us at the address in section 11 and we will delete it.

Deleting your account is not a request for a refund. To ask for a Deluxe refund, do so within the 14 days described in the Terms, before you delete.

7. Retention

We keep account and synced fitness data for as long as your account exists, so the app can work for you across devices. When you delete your account, the data described in section 6 is erased immediately. We do not keep an archive of deleted accounts. Support tickets are deleted with the account that sent them. Optional anonymous analytics contain no account identifier and are used only in aggregate. Data stored only on your device remains under your device or browser controls until you clear it.

8. Security

Gymplified uses Supabase authentication, email verification, access controls and database row-level security for supported account data, and encrypts data in transit. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and use a unique password. If a personal-data breach is likely to put you at risk, we will notify you and the Data Protection Commission as the law requires.

9. Your privacy rights

If the GDPR or similar law applies to you, you have the right to:

  • access your personal information and get a copy (use the in-app export, or ask us);
  • rectify inaccurate information (edit it in the app, or ask us);
  • erase your information (see section 6);
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, meaning a machine-readable copy (the JSON backup and CSV export in Settings → Data);
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent (section 4);
  • complain to a data-protection authority. In Ireland this is the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

You can use the in-app controls or contact us at the address in section 11. Exercising your rights is free. We will reply within one month, and may ask you to confirm your identity first. In the app we can only act on information we hold, and we cannot identify anonymous analytics events as yours.

10. Children and age requirements

Gymplified is intended for people aged 16 or over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone younger. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. International processing

The Gymplified database and storage are hosted in the EU (Ireland). Some of our providers, such as GitHub (website hosting) and Stripe, may process limited information in countries outside the European Economic Area. Where information is transferred outside the EEA, we rely on providers that use approved transfer mechanisms where the law requires them, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

12. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as Gymplified adds features or changes providers. The current version and update date will be published on this page, and we will give notice in the app of material changes. For privacy questions or to exercise any right, use the in-app Help & Support feature or email gymplifiedsupport@gmail.com.
