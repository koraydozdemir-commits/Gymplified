Data

Backup

A backup is one file with everything: workouts, nutrition, records and settings. Without an account, your data lives only in this browser.

Workout history CSV

Export workout history as a portable CSV, or import workout-history CSV exports from other gym apps. CSV backups always use kg and km so they restore exactly.

Supports Hevy, Strong, FitNotes and standard exercise/set CSVs. Imported history is merged without replacing existing logs.
